Word Café: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The short version

Word Café does not collect, send, share, or sell any personal data. It has no account, no ads, and no analytics. It does not use the internet.

What is stored on your phone

The app saves your game progress (levels finished, words you have learned, quotes and puzzles unlocked, your streak, and any memory tricks you type yourself) in the app's private storage on your phone. This data never leaves your phone. If you uninstall the app or choose "Erase my progress", it is deleted.

The app may also keep one extra safety copy of your progress on your phone: before an update changes how progress is saved, before you restore a backup code (so you can undo it), or if saved data is found damaged. These copies stay on your phone too, and "Erase my progress" removes them.

Backup codes

You can make a backup code, which is plain text describing your progress. The app does not upload it. You decide where to keep it. It contains no name, email, or device information. If you wrote your own memory tricks, they are part of the code, so do not type private details into them.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions that need your approval. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Children

The app is made for students and adults preparing for exams such as the GRE. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
